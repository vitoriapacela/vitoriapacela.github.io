I have been practicing yoga since 2016, and I have recently completed a 380-hour Hatha and Vinyasa teacher training with [Sylvie Tremblay](https://yoga-sangha.com/en/pages/sylvie-tremblay?srsltid=AfmBOooTHky2FuoBwcbESc7OQSYnA_HWKrbGpR0ookIQ_TDEmyJjUHJn) at Yoga Sangha. I'm available to teach on-demand, and I can also offer yoga classes at AI/academic conferences and workshops.

Yoga is my way of rediscovering myself and connecting with life. It is the practice that guides my existence in this world and enriches it with love. I am grateful to Sylvie for the teachings she has transmitted to me, as well as to multiple teachers and mentors. I'm fortunate to grow together with an authentic community.

On a research note, I'm interested in studying consciousness and working on the computational and mathematical fundamentals of yogic philosophy, with the purpose of better understanding and validating the tradition.
